EHX Attack-Decay

Tape Reverse Simulation Pedal
[ Gear Photo Slot ]

Notes: An unwieldy and experimental effect. It simulates a reversed tape swell by allowing control over audio attck and decay values. It also has a fuzz built in, making it my only analog fuzz. I bought this from a bandmate, hard to say who's gotten more use out of it.
